Getting testnet ETH

GIWA Sepolia gas is paid in ETH. Bridge Sepolia ETH or use the GIWA faucet, then wrap a small amount into WETH to trade on curves.
Aromma is an independent community launchpad on GIWA Sepolia testnet (chain 91342), not operated by Ammora Labs. Do not use real funds. Contracts, quotes and transaction plans come from @ammora-protocol/sdk. Launch history is read from the LaunchFactory event log via the GIWA explorer.
